Nutrigenomics

The same meal does not land the same way in two bodies.

Nutrigenomics is the study of why. Not allergies, not a fad diet — the ordinary, measurable differences in how people absorb, convert and clear what they eat. One person turns the omega-3 in flaxseed into the form their brain uses; the next converts barely half of it and needs the fish instead. Both are normal. The difference is written at a handful of known places in the genome, and unlike almost everything else in health, those places never change. Read once, useful for the rest of your life.

What we read

63 genes. 72 loci. Nothing else leaves the parser.

Every marker on the panel earns its place through replicated diet-interaction evidence, and each is weighted by how strong that evidence actually is — strong, moderate, or emerging. Two of them matter especially here.

rs671 · ALDH2

Alcohol sensitivity

Sharply reduces the enzyme that clears acetaldehyde — the compound behind flushing, nausea and headache. Common across East Asian populations and near-absent elsewhere. It is a physical signal that less is genuinely better for you, not a preference.

rs4988235 · MCM6

Lactose tolerance

Keeps the lactase gene switched on into adulthood. Most adults worldwide stop producing lactase after childhood, and that is the normal human pattern rather than a defect — so most users here read low, and we frame it as typical.

Privacy architecture

Four mechanisms. None of them relied on alone.

Most services promise to guard your genome. We built a pipeline that never holds one long enough to guard.

01

Architectural

The parser streams your file and retains only the 72 panel loci. A 640,000-marker export never exists in memory as a whole, so there is nothing substantial to leak.

02

Buffer hygiene

The upload buffer is zeroed when the request exits — including on exception paths, which is where this is usually forgotten.

03

Log redaction

A filter strips rsID and genotype pairs from every log record, installed on the root logger before the first request is served. The likeliest leak is an exception message, not a database.

04

Response guard

Every response is checked for genotype content before it is sent — structurally, for forbidden keys and bulk marker maps, and textually.

Not encrypted-and-kept. Not retained.

Your file is read in an isolated session and discarded before the response is sent. What persists is the derived trait results — a lossy, one-way projection you cannot reconstruct a genome from. You can delete those in one tap, at any time.

Every recommendation supports wellness and lifestyle decisions, never medical diagnosis. Panel and model updates are versioned, so any result can be traced back to the knowledge base that produced it.
